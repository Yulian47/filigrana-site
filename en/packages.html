Questions about plans

Buying or renting — common questions.

What is the difference between buying and renting?

When you buy, you pay a one-time fixed price and own the finished website. When you rent, you pay a monthly fee from 499 NOK with free creation and hosting, SSL and support included, for a minimum of 12 months.

Is creation really free when I rent?

Yes, there is no setup fee. Each plan includes a set number of pages, services and monthly changes. Work beyond that is billed separately, for example an extra page from 500 NOK or an extra hour for 800 NOK.

What happens after the first 12 months?

The agreement continues month by month. After the minimum period you can cancel with one month's notice.

Can I change plan or add services later?

Yes. You can move to a larger plan or add services such as extra pages, another language, copywriting or SEO whenever you need them.
